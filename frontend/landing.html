REASONING PIPELINE

The Decision Support Workflow

How Trademark DSS structures raw dispute facts into actionable, law-grounded decision frameworks.

1
Dispute Intake
Parties, trademark, contractual context, and narrative facts are captured and validated.
2
Arbitrability Check
Deterministic Booz Allen and Vidya Drolia tests evaluate statutory jurisdiction.
3
Precedent Retrieval
SVM classifier and ChromaDB retrieve binding and persuasive Indian case law.
4
Legal Provisions
Trade Marks Act and Arbitration Act sections are identified and mapped to facts.
5
Issue Formulation
Four distinct legal issues with statutory options are framed for determination.
6
Adversarial Analysis
Balanced claimant arguments, respondent defenses, and alternative options are computed.
7
Citation Verification
Every judicial citation is validated against authoritative precedent registries.
8
Structured DOCX
A complete 7-section Word report is compiled and delivered for arbitrator review.
ETHICAL & REGULATORY GUARDRAILS

Human-in-the-Loop Decision Support

Trademark DSS is an assistive analytical instrument. It is designed to empower human legal judgment, not replace it.

  • Decision Support Only: This system assists the arbitrator or legal counsel. It does not issue binding awards or legal advice.
  • Curated Indian Law Precedents: Analysis is grounded in Indian statutes and landmark judgments from the Supreme Court and High Courts.
  • Citation Verification & Unverified Markers: Any citation not present in our indexed registry is visibly marked with [UNVERIFIED].
  • Fallback Generation Transparency: If cloud LLM services are unavailable, deterministic fallbacks trigger and are clearly labeled on the interface and generated documents.
