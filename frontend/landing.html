ETHICAL & REGULATORY GUARDRAILS

Human-in-the-Loop Decision Support

Trademark DSS is an assistive analytical instrument. It is designed to empower human legal judgment, not replace it.

Gated Operational Protocol
  • Decision Support Only: Operates strictly as an assistive analytical tool for arbitrators and counsel. It does not issue binding awards, judicial rulings, or autonomous verdicts.
  • Not Formal Legal Advice: Statutory summaries, issue formulations, and analyses are informational and do not substitute for formal legal counsel or create an attorney-client relationship.
  • Human Judgment Required: Human-in-the-loop oversight is strictly required. Legal practitioners retain sole professional responsibility for evaluating evidence and drawing conclusions.
  • Authorities Must Be Verified: All statutory citations and precedents must be verified against official court records. Unindexed citations are visibly flagged as [UNVERIFIED].
  • Uncertainty & Conflicting Evidence: When facts present ambiguous arbitration clauses or conflicting trademark rights, the system flags doctrinal uncertainty and supplies balanced adversarial positions.
  • Fallback Generation Warnings: If cloud LLM services are unreachable, deterministic rule-based fallbacks trigger automatically and are prominently labeled on both interface and exported reports.
